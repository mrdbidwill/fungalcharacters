Thanks!

fungalcharacters.org stands on work by many people. This page keeps the list, and it grows as people help.

Most of fungalcharacters.org’s characters began with nolo_mode’s list “Characters With Taxonomic Value For Stipitate-Lamellate Mushrooms”, first posted on Mushroom Observer in 2019; a shorter version is the iNaturalist guide “Intro To Identifying Fungi”. nolo_mode gave permission to use it in November 2026. nolo_mode has also started myceliome.com, a mushroom community for the people of Thailand (open to visitors in Thailand).

nolo_mode in turn credits the people and places the list grew from:

  • Gastón Guzmán (1932–2016), Mexican mycologist, whose literature shaped it.
  • Alan Rockefeller, through personal correspondence.
  • Else Vellinga, for her glossary in Flora Agaricina Neerlandica vol. 1 (Bas, Kuyper, Noordeloos & Vellinga, eds, 1988), which nolo_mode recommends to others.
  • Mushroom Observer, where the list was first shared.

And many others. Thank you.
