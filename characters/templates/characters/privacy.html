{% translate "What is stored about you" %}

  • {% translate "Your username, which is public." %}
  • {% translate "Your email address, which is not shown publicly and never sold. It is used to sign in (with your username or email), to confirm your account, to reset your password and to tell you about changes to your account's security. It is passed to Resend only so that these emails can be delivered. There are no newsletters." %}
  • {% translate "Your password, stored only in a form that cannot be turned back into the password." %}
  • {% translate "If you turn on two-factor sign-in: its secret key and your recovery codes." %}
  • {% translate "When you joined, when you last signed in, and when you agreed to the contribution terms (and which version)." %}
  • {% translate "For each signed-in device: the browser, IP address and last activity, so you can see and sign out other devices on your account page. Security emails about your account also show the time, IP address and browser of the change." %}
  • {% translate "If you send a “Needs review” report: your note, the page, the time and, if you are signed in, your username. It is not stored with your IP address." %}
  • {% translate "To limit heavy use of the report form and the API, your IP address is used only in scrambled form, as a counter that is deleted after an hour or less." %}
  • {% translate "Cookies: for signing in and security, for one-time notices on the next page, and to remember your choice of language and of common or scientific names. No advertising, analytics or tracking cookies; no scripts or fonts from other companies." %}

{% translate "Where it is kept" %}

  • {% translate "The site runs on a server rented from Hostinger, in the United States. Email is sent through Resend, in the United States." %}
  • {% translate "The server's web logs (IP address, page requested, browser) are kept for 14 days. The site's own logs (time, page requested, browser) are limited in size and overwritten as new entries arrive." %}
  • {% translate "The database is backed up every night; the server keeps 30 days of backups, and the administrator keeps a copy off the server." %}
  • {% translate "When something goes wrong, the site emails an error report to its administrator through Resend. It can include your username, IP address, browser, the page and what you had just submitted (passwords, codes and keys are hidden)." %}
